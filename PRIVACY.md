# Privacy policy: Chat Exporter for Character.AI

Last updated: November 3, 2026. Published by ANIMA AI 007 SERVICES - FZCO, the company behind Amavie (amavie.ai).

**We do not collect, receive, store, sell or share any of your data through this extension.** We have no server for it, and
it contains no analytics.

- **What it reads.** Only after you press its button on a Character.AI tab, it reads your own chats, characters, personas and
  memory boxes from Character.AI's own web API, using the session you are already signed in with in that tab.
- **Where it goes.** The data is written to a file that your browser saves on your device. It is not sent anywhere else.
- **Your session.** The session token is read from the page while the export runs, sent only to Character.AI, and never saved.
- **What stays behind.** Nothing. When you close the tab, the extension keeps no copy.
- **The viewer link.** The panel offers a link to our free viewer at amavie.ai. It opens only if you press it, and the
  extension passes it no data. If you then open your file there, the viewer reads it in your browser; Amavie's own
  [privacy policy](https://amavie.ai/privacy) covers that website.

Questions: support@amavie.ai.
